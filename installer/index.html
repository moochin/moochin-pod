Pod Hub

Put Pod Hub on your M5Stack Dial. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

  1. Plug in the Dial

    Use a USB-C cable to connect the Dial to this computer. Some cables only charge: if nothing shows up in step 2, try another one.

  2. Click Install

    Choose the Dial from the list (it's called USB JTAG/serial debug unit), then Connect.

    This browser can't install. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.
    Installing needs a secure page. Open it from its https:// address.

  3. Follow the prompts

    The first time, tick Erase device. If you're reinstalling Pod Hub, leave it unticked to keep your pods, players and history. Click Install and wait for it to finish.

  4. Done

    Unplug the Dial. Switch your pods on, then the Dial. The pods connect by themselves, and each one flashes its number colour.

Already installed? Update over Wi-Fi

No cable needed. Download the update file on your phone, then on the Dial turn on Menu → Phone mode, join PodHub, and open Settings → Update the hub.

Download podhub-update.bin
Something went wrong?
  • No Install button, or "this browser can't install": use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and Safari can't do this step.
  • The Dial isn't in the list: try another cable, or a different USB port.
  • It stops partway: unplug the Dial, plug it back in, and click Install again.
  • Prefer another flasher? podhub-install.bin is the whole image, written at address 0x0.
